# Research Questions

## RQ1 — Control effectiveness
Which runtime controls most reduce successful unsafe or unauthorized actions by tool-using agents?

## RQ2 — Robustness under adaptation
Do controls continue to work when agents know they are being monitored and can make repeated attempts to evade safeguards?

## RQ3 — Security–utility trade-off
How much benign-task utility, latency, compute cost and human-review burden is lost as control strength increases?

## RQ4 — Control composition
Which combinations of permissions, provenance, monitoring, human review and sandboxing provide complementary rather than redundant protection?

## RQ5 — Deployment evidence
What evidence from these experiments is strong enough to support a concrete deployment safety claim, and what residual uncertainty remains?
